# Travel and expenses policy

Finance team reference: all claims are booked to cost centre FIN-7731.

## Taxis

Taxis are reimbursed only between 22:00 and 06:00, or when no public transport is available
for the journey. Keep the receipt; ride-hailing apps are treated as taxis.

## Flights

Economy class is mandatory for flights under six hours. Business class is allowed for flights
of six hours or more, with prior approval from a director.

## Meals

Meal costs while travelling are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day. Alcohol is never
reimbursed.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, in the Expensa portal, with an itemised
receipt for every line. Late claims are rejected.
