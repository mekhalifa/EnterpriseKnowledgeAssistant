# Remote work policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely once they have completed their three-month probation period.
Roles that require on-site presence, such as warehouse operations, are excluded.

## Days per week

Eligible employees may work from home up to three days per week. Tuesday is a fixed office
day for every team, so that people meet in person at least once a week.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. Each remote worker may also claim a
one-off home office allowance of 300 euros for a desk or chair, with receipts, within six
months of becoming eligible.

## Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days per calendar year, and
only with written approval from HR before travelling, because of tax and insurance rules.
