# IT security policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Store
them in Vaultly, the company password manager; never in documents, chat messages or browser
notes. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email and remote access.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the service desk within one hour, on extension
4400 or at servicedesk@northwind.example. The device will be locked and wiped remotely. Also
report it to the police if it was stolen, and send the report number to the service desk.

## Phishing

Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. Use the "Report phishing" button
in your email client; the security team reviews every report within one working day.
