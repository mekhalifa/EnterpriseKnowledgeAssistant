# Salary bands

Confidential: HR only.

## Bands

Band A covers entry-level roles, from EUR 38,000 to 46,500 per year.
Band B covers experienced specialists, from EUR 47,000 to 60,900 per year.
Band C covers senior specialists and team leads, from EUR 61,750 to 74,200 per year.
Band D covers managers, from EUR 75,000 to 98,000 per year.

## Annual review

Salaries are reviewed every April. Increases within a band depend on the performance review
and are capped at eight percent per year.
